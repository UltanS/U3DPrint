ABOUT U3D

From an idea to something real.

U3D Prints is my independent 3D printing project, built around making useful, interesting and personalised objects.

Why U3D Prints?

I started U3D as a way to turn 3D printing from a hobby into something I could build on.

What began with experimenting, calibrating printers and making one-off objects has grown into designing and selling custom prints. The main focus right now is LED lightboxes, while I continue developing new products and improving the quality of every print.

I handle the process from preparing the design and slicing the model through to printing, assembly and finishing. Because it’s small-batch work, I can experiment and give each order individual attention.
